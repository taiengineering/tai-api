## Cross-industry inventory taxonomy to investigate
- Safety governance: policy, objectives, resource allocation, personnel appointment, joint participation.
- Risk workflow: hazard identification, methods-based risk assessment, controls, verification/closeout.
- Job planning and authorization: task-specific plans, work permits, pre-job briefings, isolation/lockout, handoff.
- Equipment/facility: master equipment records, condition checks, inspections, maintenance work log, repairs, statutory inspection evidence.
- Chemicals/environment: material register, SDS reference/change, inventories, labels, exposure, storage, spills and response.
- Workers/health: training planning and execution, competency, PPE issue/return, health and exposure programs.
- Contractors: prequalification, coordination, permits, work area turnover, safety review and deficiency closure.
- Incidents and emergency: near miss, initial notification, investigation, preventive measures, drill history.
- Industry variants: industry/work-task constraints, never manufacture legal obligations by industry label alone.
**Categories are editorial research hypotheses**; each actual form must be supported by specific source and have independently defined field lists.
